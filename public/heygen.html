HeyGen real-time: LiveAvatar

HeyGen has a real-time product, now called LiveAvatar. HeyGen's docs say the old Interactive Avatar / Streaming Avatar product was sunsetting on 31 March 2026 and has moved to LiveAvatar, so ignore the older Streaming Avatar SDK tutorials you'll find.

How it works

LiveAvatar is a single API with two modes:

  • FULL mode: HeyGen runs everything: speech recognition, the LLM, the voice and turn-taking.
  • LITE mode: HeyGen only renders the avatar, and you plug in your own LLM and voice stack. It costs half the credits of FULL.

Paid plans have no concurrency cap. HeyGen says the median time to the first frame is under 300 ms, with 99.99% API uptime. Those numbers come from HeyGen's own blog, which also ranks its product first, so test them yourself.

For this project

  • No RunPod needed. HeyGen hosts all of it, and the video streams to the browser over WebRTC.
  • LITE mode fits if you want the avatar driven by your own agent, such as Hermes or your Claude setup, rather than HeyGen's LLM.
  • Trade-off against H3 Max: a real-time avatar is built for low latency, not the cinematic quality of a diffusion clip. A common setup is a LiveAvatar for live conversation and H3 Max for pre-rendered promo or talking-head clips.

To try it, there is a 14-day trial with 3 minutes of streaming. Check current per-minute pricing on HeyGen's site before you build around it.
